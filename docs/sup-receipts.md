# Receipts: LUC solar special-use-permit dockets

Docket-by-docket source links for the claim that solar special use permits pass
the Land Use Commission unanimously and unopposed. Full coding and context:
`notes/sup-census.md` and `data/sup_census.csv`. Verified 2026-07-27.

## Verdict on the report claim

The claim as drafted — "Of eight special use permit applications that have come
before the Land Use Commission **on Oʻahu**, seven have been approved
unanimously without intervenors, and one is currently under review" — is
**wrong on geography**. The count of eight is **statewide**, not Oʻahu. The
eight dockets span three islands: Oʻahu 4, Kauaʻi 3, Maui 1. The one still under
review (SP26-417) is on **Maui**.

Two accurate rewrites:

- **Statewide framing:** "Of the eight solar special use permit applications
  that have come before the Land Use Commission statewide, seven have been
  approved unanimously and without intervenors; the eighth — Kūihelani Solar
  Phase 2, on Maui — is under review as of July 2026."
- **Oʻahu framing:** "All four solar special use permit applications from Oʻahu
  that reached the Land Use Commission were approved unanimously and without
  intervenors; none is under review."

Both facts are exact against the record below: 7 of 7 decided dockets were
unanimous approvals with zero intervenors, and the 4 Oʻahu dockets were all
unanimous approvals with zero intervenors.

## Notes on the receipts

- "Unanimous" reflects the recorded LUC vote; the sub-8 tallies are excused or
  vacant seats, not dissents (SP15-406 had two vacant seats; SP17-408 two
  excused; SP26-416 one excused, one recused — a sitting KIUC director — and one
  vacant). No solar SP docket has ever drawn a dissenting LUC vote.
- "No intervenors" means no party sought or was admitted to intervene at the LUC
  in any docket (`notes/sup-census.md`, Intervenors section). Opposition
  testimony is nearly absent and never rose to intervention; the only successful
  organized opposition to a Hawaii solar land-use permit was Paeahu (Maui), in a
  county forum, not at the LUC.
- The docket list is the complete LUC-tier population (`notes/sup-census.md`,
  Coverage and gaps): the statewide SP numbering was enumerated 1963–2026 and
  every 2005–2026 title inspected. Docket #410 is absent from the sequence
  (plausibly a withdrawn application, not solar); it is not a missing solar
  docket.
- Why only large landowners appear, and why the LUC stage is not where solar is
  screened, is analyzed in `notes/sup-census.md` (the SUP is required only above
  the cap on B/C soils; D/E and urban/federal solar skips this tier).
