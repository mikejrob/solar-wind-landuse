About the data

  • Ag-district parcels: TMK parcels intersecting the State Agricultural District (geodata.hawaii.gov), with attributes compiled in the repository: owner of record (Honolulu RPAD, tax year 2027, entity-resolved), LSB soil-class acres, solar-eligible acres under HRS §205-4.5(a)(20) (S0: min(10% of parcel, 20 ac, B/C area)) and a 20%-no-hard-cap counterfactual (S3), HDOA 2020 use, HCDL 2024 crop cover, slope shares (USGS 3DEP), distance to mapped 46 kV and 138 kV lines (HIFLD/OSM; 46 kV under-mapped), reviewer issue flags, and HDOA-vs-HCDL change-candidate status (UNVERIFIED).
  • LSB classes: Land Study Bureau overall productivity ratings A (best) to E. Solar is banned on A; capped on B/C; uncapped on D/E.
  • HDOA 2020 baseline: Perroy & Collier (2022), UH Hilo SDAV for the Hawaiʻi Dept. of Agriculture; commercial operations digitized from 2018–20 imagery. Seed-production polygons are gross operational footprints (about a quarter planted at any time).
  • Cropland Data Layer: USDA NASS / UH Mānoa Hawaiʻi CDL V2.1, 10 m annual classification (AlphaEarth embeddings + random forest). 2024 class F1 0.90–1.00; 2025 unassessed. Grassland/pasture is a cover class, not fenced pasture.
  • Existing solar: OpenStreetMap plant polygons (incomplete). Military: DoD fee land and 2029 state-lease parcels. All parcels: fetched live from the state ParcelsZoning service at zoom 15+.
  • Acreages are GIS estimates. Nothing here is a survey or a regulatory determination. Issue flags are labels, not exclusions.
