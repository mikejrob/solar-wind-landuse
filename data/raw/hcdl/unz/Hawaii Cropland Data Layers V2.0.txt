Hawaii Cropland Data Layers (HCDL) V2.0 
Zhe Li 
Abstract: 
The Hawaii Cropland Data Layer (HCDL) is the first-ever raster, geo-referenced, crop-specific land 
cover data layer for Hawaii. Unlike the 10-meter resolution CDL for CONUS or HCDL V1.x, which 
utilized 10-day image composites of surface reflectance data across multiple spectral bands and 
NDVI, crop classification for HCDL 2023 and 2024 V2.0 leveraged the 64-dimensional geospatial 
"embeddings" from the Satellite Embedding dataset developed by Google DeepMind's new 
geospatial AI model, AlphaEarth Foundations. This dataset was derived from multiple datastreams, 
including optical, radar, LiDAR, and other sources. 
Ground-reference data for crop type classification was developed based on satellite/aerial imagery 
interpretation and field surveys conducted by the University of Hawaii at Manoa from December 
2023 through October 2024. Fieldwork covered the islands of Kauai, Oahu, Maui, Molokai, and the 
Big Island. HCDL 2024 was derived from 2024 embeddings using the SmileRandomForest classifier 
in Google Earth Engine, based on the 2024 ground-reference data. Since the embedding space is 
consistent across years, it can be used for condition change detection. Due to the limited ground- 
reference data available in 2023, HCDL 2023 was derived from 2023 embeddings solely based on 
the trained 2024 models, thus reducing uncertainties caused by the subjective human-involved 
training data labeling process.

HCDL aims to map major, specialty, and culturally significant crops in Hawaii, as shown in Table 1. 
The "Other Exotic Fruits" category encompasses a variety of tropical fruits such as mango, litchi, 
longan, cacao, dragon fruit, jackfruit, breadfruit, and rambutan. The " Misc Vegs & Fruits " category 
includes tomato, pumpkin, turmeric, mustard, eggplant and others.
