CroplandCROS and Cropland Data Layer

CroplandCROS | FAQ’s | Metadata | National Download | CDL Viewer | Hawaii CDL Viewer

Announcements

Announcement: The 2025 Hawaii Cropland Data Layer (HCDL) was released June 12, 2026. The USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service (NASS) produced the HCDL at a 10-meter spatial resolution for the years 2023 and 2024 which were originally released to the public August 25, 2025. Both 2023 and 2024 were reprocessed using improved training input and released alongside the new 2025 Hawaii CDL on June 12, 2026. All years of data are available for download on the 'National Download' webpage. Read more in the official Hawaii CDL Press Release.

Announcement: The 2025 Cropland Data Layer was released February 27, 2026. The CDL spatial resolution has been increased from 30-meter to 10-meter beginning in 2024. The data will soon be available on the CroplandCROS web app. A 30-meter nearest neighbor resampled version is also available for download for consistency with historical CDL data.

Announcement: The new ArcGIS StoryMap, 'Cropland Data Layer: Overview, history, and modernization of mapping croplands across the U.S.,' details the evolution of the CDL program and allows users to view and interact with the most current year of data.

Announcement: New CDL Viewer. The Agricultural Cropland Tracking and Interactive Visualization Environment (ACTIVE) is a web application based on Google Earth Engine. It is designed for the rapid visualization of USDA NASS Cropland Data Layers (CDL). Apart from crop acreage assessment, this app offers unique functions such as visualizing crop temporal entropy, specific crop types, and conducting crop-specific land cover change detection. Users can conduct crop type inquiries from both present and historical datasets and visualize crop phenology through time-series NDVI. Additionally, users can overlay near real-time earth observation data for field verification.
