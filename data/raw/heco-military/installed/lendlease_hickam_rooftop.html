Oahu, Hawaii, United States

Hickam Communities Rooftop Solar

Hickam Communities LLC (HC), owned and managed by Lendlease, is the preferred developer for the US Air Force's privatized military residential community located at Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam on Oahu.

 
 

Project at a glance

 
 

Hickam Communities Rooftop Solar

Client
SolarCity

Location
Oahu, Hawaii, United States

Completion
2013

 

Area
4.7 megawatts

Assignments
Originated, Co-Developed and Served as Off-Taker

 

Project outcome

The project scope includes the development, design and construction of 1,208 new homes, renovation of 1,451 existing homes, and installation of 4.7MW of rooftop photovoltaic systems.

The solar power generated will offset approximately 7 million pounds of CO2 annually.

Through a power purchase agreement (PPA), SolarCity engineered, installed and will maintain the solar systems, which will be interconnected by Hawaiian Electric Company Lendlease represented Hickam Communities in the approval, build-out, commissioning, interconnection, and operations of the PPA.
