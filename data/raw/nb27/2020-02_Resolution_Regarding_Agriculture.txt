 
North Shore Neighborhood Board No. 27 
Resolution Regarding Agriculture 
 
WHEREAS, the area now called the North Shore was known in ancient times as ‘āina momona, or fattened 
land and it was the breadbasket of Oʻahu; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the State Constitution states, “the State shall conserve and protect agricultural lands, promote 
diversified agriculture, increase agricultural self-sufficiency and assure the availability of agriculturally suitable 
lands.” 1; and 
 
WHEREAS, many people from Honolulu and around the world are attracted to the beautiful rural setting and 
lifestyle of the North Shore, thus possibly creating conflicts between some residents and existing farming 
operations; and 
 
WHEREAS, we support the collaboration and coexistence of all farming businesses in order to keep the country 
country and promote diversity in the agricultural community; and 
 
WHEREAS, we support all responsible farming practices including best management practices and integrated 
pest management programs and encourage farmers to be good neighbors; and 
 
WHEREAS, we recognize that the State Department of Agriculture is the appropriate state agency to regulate 
agricultural activities; and 
 
THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that prime Ag lands be preserved and used in a sustainable manner for 
agricultural purposes, local food production, and accessory ag purposes.  Renewable energy projects shall be 
limited to those that make the farm’s or agriculture company’s day-to-day operations more economically viable; 
and                                                                                                                                          
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the North Shore Neighborhood Board supports the right of farmers and 
agricultural businesses to farm their lands within existing laws; and  
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that we encourage the Hawai’i Department of Agriculture and the University of 
Hawai’i, College of Tropical Agriculture and Human Resources to provide educational opportunities and 
Cooperative Extension support to farmers in agricultural practices which foster the sustainable growth of our 
agriculture sector and food security; and 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the state and county develop appropriate laws, policies and budget 
allocations to promote vigorous enforcement and prosecution of agricultural trespassing, theft and vandalism to 
protect farmers and agricultural businesses; and    
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that we urge the State Legislature to fund additional positions within the 
Department of Agriculture, to help protect and enhance Hawai’i agriculture, including positions for Agriculture 
Resource Management, plant and animal industries, marketing, invasive species control, food safety education 
and compliance, and farmer education; and  
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the state Water Commission and Board of Water Supply ensure that 
adequate water resources are allocated to maintain and increase agricultural production to the fullest extent 
possible and prevent diversion of North Shore water resources to urban development; and  
 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the County complete the wastewater plant improvements at Wahiawa to 
prevent any more sewage spills, so that Lake Wilson water can be used for growing all food crops on North 
Shore Ag lands; and  
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Dept. of Education ensure that there are educational programs and 
experience in the schools to encourage interest in science, agriculture, and agribusiness and that the Department 
of Labor develop workforce policies for agriculture workers while also addressing agriculture workforce 
housing; and  
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the State and the County develop policies, resources and opportunities for 
farmers to obtain land for new and existing farmers, while ensuring land use policies that expedite affordable, 
long-term leases to farmers and agricultural producers so that they have a stable environment within which to 
operate and obtain financing; and 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that agriculture lands will not be CPR’d or otherwise divided without a major 
permit and public hearing(s); and 
  
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED that the Secretary is directed to transmit copies of this resolution to the 
Governor, Senate, House of Representatives, the Chair of the Dept. of Agriculture, the Mayor, the Chair and 
members of the City Council, the Water Commission, Board of Water Supply, UH College of Tropical Ag and 
Human Resources, and the Department of Education.  
 
This resolution has been duly discussed, considered, and voted upon in the affirmative by the North Shore 
Neighborhood Board on 2/25/2020.
