NORTH SHORE NEIGHBORHOOD BOARD NO. 27
North Shore Neighborhood Board Resolution Regarding Windmill Farms

WHEREAS, the North Shore of Oahu is the site of a windmill farm of 30 windmills in Kawailoa above the historic Waimea Valley;

WHEREAS, a large segment of the community is upset by the visual blight this project has caused on the landscape of the North Shore, renowned for its beauty and amazing view planes, and especially at Waimea Valley;

WHEREAS, Hawaii's primary industry is tourism which is predicated on the visual beauty of the islands;

WHEREAS, windmills are not a reliable source of energy so much so that fossil-fuel plants must run constantly to back up wind projects, sometimes consuming more fuel and creating more CO2 than if power is generated directly with fossil fuels.

WHEREAS, windmills have negative impacts on tourism, property values, the environment, especially birds, bats, and other wildlife, and sometimes human health (noise and the blinking lights) to the point that other countries like Britain have stopped further windmill projects;

WHEREAS, the height limit for resort-zoned land on the North Shore is 90 feet, but the height of the windmills on agriculture land is 480 feet;

BE IT RESOLVED that the North Shore Neighborhood Board advocate that no more windmill projects be built in Hawaii, and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Environmental Impact Statement process be amended to ensure that those properties and people directly affected by a project be directly informed of the impacts with visual simulations equal to what the human eye sees;

BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED that the Secretary is directed to transmit copies of this motion to the Governor, Senate, House of Representatives, Hawaii State Energy Office, the Chair and members of the City Council, the Mayor, the Director of Planning and Permitting, and the Planning Commission of the City and County of Honolulu.

This resolution has been duly discussed, considered, and voted upon in the affirmative by elected members of the North Shore Neighborhood Board.

Signed: Mike Lyons, Chair. Date: February 26, 2013
